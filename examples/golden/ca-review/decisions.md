# Decision log

> One line per decision: `date · from X to Y · because Z`.
> This is the object axis — why it is the way it is, not what happened that day.
> Append-only, with one exception: a factual error is corrected in place, and the
> correction itself gets a line (M4b).

## Protocol

- 2026-09-15 · domain created · because the owner asked for: a second opinion before committing to something

- 2026-09-22 · added "evidence must be named" · because a review without sources cannot be re-checked six months later
- 2026-10-02 · from "recommendation" to "one dated verdict" · because recommendations accumulate and verdicts close
