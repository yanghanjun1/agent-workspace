# Should the evening routine move earlier, or stay where it is?
decides: whether to move the deep-work block from 21:00 to 19:00 · owner: the owner · date: 2026-10-18

## Evidence

- `ca-review/log.md` 2026-10-18: three of the last five deep-work blocks started after 22:00.
- `examples/golden/ca-reading/1-data/2026-10.jsonl`: reading sessions cluster late; two of four have `mood: drifting`.
- `ca-review/decisions.md` 2026-09-22: reviews without named evidence were re-litigated twice.

## Passes

### A — argue for moving it earlier

Starting at 19:00 puts the block before the point where the day's accumulated load shows
up in the records. The two "drifting" reading sessions are the cost of the current slot,
and they are measurable rather than felt.

### B — argue against moving it earlier

19:00 is when the household is loudest. The current slot is late not by preference but
because it is the first hour that is uninterrupted; moving it earlier may trade a
low-quality hour for no hour at all.

### C — assume it is now six months later and the move failed; why

It failed because the move was made once and never revisited: the first two weeks were
better, the interruption pattern changed, and nobody re-measured. The failure was not the
hour, it was treating a schedule change as a decision instead of an experiment.

## Where they disagree

A and B disagree about whether the current slot is a constraint or a habit. That is
settleable with data the owner already has: for two weeks, note whether 19:00 was
interrupted, without changing anything.

C does not disagree with either; it changes what "success" means.

## Verdict

- 2026-10-18 · run the 19:00 slot for two weeks as an experiment, log interruptions daily, and re-review on 2026-11-01; do not decide the permanent hour now.
