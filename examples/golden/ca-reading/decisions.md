# Decision log

> One line per decision: `date · from X to Y · because Z`.
> This is the object axis — why it is the way it is, not what happened that day.
> Append-only, with one exception: a factual error is corrected in place, and the
> correction itself gets a line (M4b). A log that protects its own errors is useless.

## Record format

- 2026-10-14 · domain created · because the owner asked for: track what I read each week and how far into each book I am
- 2026-10-14 · from "free text in a notes file" to "one JSON line per session" · because a weekly total cannot be computed from prose
- 2026-10-16 · from "ebooks use pages" to "ebooks use loc" · because page numbers do not exist in a reflowable book

## Week boundary

- 2026-10-16 · from "Sunday" to "Monday" · because weekend reading belongs to the week that just ended
